# Market Research — Organic Food & F-Commerce in Bangladesh (2025–2026)

Companion to PRD.md. Purpose: decide WHAT organic food to sell, who the f-commerce players are, and where the gap is.

---

## 1. Market sizing & momentum (from research)

- **Organic food market growth (Bangladesh):** ~6% (2021) → 8.2% (2025) CAGR; fastest-growing food sub-segment (6Wresearch, Mariners Group).

- **Demand driver:** fear of pesticides, hormones, formalin in conventional food; rising UHNI health-consciousness; expat/diplomatic preference for chemical-free.

- **Online is now the #1 channel:** in a Dhaka study (227 respondents), **66.5% bought organic food online** — higher than any single physical retailer (Meena Bazar, Kazi & Kazi, Health Food Stores).

- **Buyer segments:** health-conscious consumers, families, premium shoppers, fitness enthusiasts. Monthly income & household size (not age/education) drive spend.

- **Online food delivery** growing ~18% YoY; health/plant-based/gluten-free/low-fat searched by 20%+ of consumers (2023).

## 3. The GAP (key insight)

**Most regular sellers = RAW PANTRY STAPLES, not cooked meals.** Many brands found (Khaas Food, Organica, Fresh Organic Foods, My Organic BD, Aranno, Olloy Organics etc.) sell raw staples — the market is very crowded.

The highest-frequency, proven organic sellers are:

1. Cold-pressed **mustard oil**
2. **Ghee** (pure/desi)
3. **Honey** (black seed, sundarban, floral)
4. **Organic rice** (Chinigura, brown)
5. **Milk / yogurt / dairy**
6. **Superfood powders** (moringa, spirulina, matcha) — fitness segment
7. **Herbal/leaf teas** (tulsi, moringa, rosemary)

These are **reorder staples** (high repeat, predictable). The market is saturated with them as *grocery*.

**What is MISSING for our target zone (Gulshan/Baridhara/DOHS/Diplomatic):**

- **Cooked, daily, ORGANIC meals delivered** to affluent gated communities. Khaas/Organica sell raw; Cookups/Ahar sell meals but not organic-premium.

- **Expat/diplomatic-specific organic meals** (continental, gluten-free, keto, diabetic, kids) — almost nobody serves this niche locally.

- **Trust + convenience bundle**: a single trusted source for both daily organic cooked meals AND the reorder staples.

**Why the gap stays open:** despite many raw-staple sellers, cooked organic daily meals for gated communities — especially the Diplomatic Zone — remain wide open. Nobody has fully claimed this space, which is our real opportunity.

## 4. Predictive assumption — WHAT we should sell

Given the gap + our affluent, time-poor, quality-obsessed TAM:

**Primary (core, high LTV):** Prepared **organic daily meals & healthy meal plans** (subscription + on-demand). This is the white space.

- Segments: healthy Bangla thali, continental/expat, keto/diabetic-friendly, kids meals.

- Use organic staples (our own/oem oil, ghee, rice, veg, meat) as the ingredient base → vertical integration story.

**Secondary (cross-sell, recurring staples):** A curated **"regular reorder" organic grocery line** using ONLY the proven high-frequency categories:

- Cold-pressed mustard oil, desi ghee, honey, organic rice, herbal teas, superfood powders, milk/yogurt.

- Sold as add-on subscription alongside meals (one cart, one trusted delivery).

**Why not compete head-on on raw grocery:** Khaas/Organica/Sunnahetc already own that with 7 outlets, apps, farms. We differentiate on *cooked organic + trust + gated-community delivery*, then attach staples for margin.
