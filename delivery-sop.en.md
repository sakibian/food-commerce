# Parcel Packing & Gated-Community Delivery SOP (Phase 1)

> Step 3 of the plan. How we pack and deliver the parcel-staples line in Gulshan / Baridhara / DOHS / Diplomatic Zone.

## 1. Packing Materials
- Use food-grade stand-up pouches, HDPE/PET bottles, laminated bosta (sack), jars.
- Weigh accurately; heat-seal every pack; print lot/batch + packing date + expiry on each.
- Hygiene: gloves, hairnet, clean dry room; no open food exposed.

## 2. Labeling (BSTI rule)
- Label MUST be in Bangla: product name, ingredients, net weight, manufacture date, expiry, our brand, contact, price.
- BSTI Certification Mark (CM) where applicable (honey, spices, oil, rice).

## 3. Order Taking
- WhatsApp Business catalog + FB/IG DM. Confirm item, pack size, qty, address, time slot.

## 4. Delivery — Inside Gated Communities
- Use OWN rider for the zone: get security gate pass / visitor ID; coordinate with guard; deliver to door in agreed window.
- Keep rider list registered with community security.
- Morning slot works best for staples.

## 5. Delivery — Outside Zone
- Pathao / REDX / Paperfly / eCourier for outside gated areas; COD or bKash advance.

## 6. Storage
- Dry goods in cool, dry room; honey/oil at room temp; never near chemicals. FIFO (old stock first).

## 7. Returns & Service
- Wrong/damaged pack replaced on next delivery; WhatsApp support; collect feedback weekly.
