# Online Presence — Where to Be to Get Organic Customers

For Gulshan / Baridhara / DOHS / Diplomatic Zone organic food business.

---

## The Reality of f-commerce in Bangladesh

Dhaka has more than 2.2 crore active Facebook users. Across the country, f-commerce delivers roughly 90,000 orders per day. The core tools of f-commerce are Facebook Pages, Groups, Messenger, and live selling. That makes Facebook our single strongest channel for this organic food business.

---

---

## Trust-building

The biggest weakness of f-commerce is trust and fear of scams. Do these every day:
- Keep a verified Facebook Page (blue tick).
- Post real customer reviews and real unboxing photos.
- Always show BSTI certification and halal badges.
- Show farm source, hygiene, and certifications on video.

---

## Priority 2 — Growth Channels

- Expat & embassy groups (e.g., InterNations Dhaka, Expats in Dhaka, embassy community groups) — for organic/expat meals.
- Local resident groups (Baridhara DOHS, Gulshan, Banani, Diplomatic Zone ladies/family groups) — staple reorders.
- LinkedIn — B2B embassy/office catering outreach.
- Chaldal / grocery apps — list packaged staples for wider reach.
- Email / community newsletter — weekly staple-box reminders.
- YouTube / short video — farm source, hygiene, certifications (trust).

---

## Delivery & Logistics

- For deliveries outside gated zones, use courier partners: Pathao, REDX, Paperfly, eCourier.
- Inside gated communities (to get past the gate), use your OWN riders — outside couriers often cannot pass the gate.
- Neighbourhood / locality cooperative pooling: pool neighbours' orders together and negotiate bulk courier rates. This is a known f-commerce best practice that lowers delivery cost.
