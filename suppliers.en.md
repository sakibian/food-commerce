# Supplier Scouting List (Contact Approach)

> Step 2 of the plan: find organic suppliers for the Phase-1 parcel-staples line.

**Strategy:** Buy bulk from verified organic suppliers / direct farms, then repack under our own brand. Target margin ≥35–40%.

---

## Sourcing Strategy
- **Wholesale first, farm later:** start with 2–3 wholesale brands for speed; add 1–2 direct farms for hero items (oil, honey, rice) to boost margin & trust.
- **Repack rights:** confirm each supplier allows resale / white-label; get bulk pack sizes.
- **Certifications:** ask for BSTI, organic, halal certificates.
- **Repack caveat:** because we repack under our own brand, confirm with every supplier that they permit white-label / resale, AND that WE obtain BSTI CM (Conformity Mark) and do Bangla labeling ourselves.

## Direct Farm / Cluster Leads
- **Mustard oil:** local cold-press (kolhu) mills — Chapai Nawabganj, Meherpur, Kushtia.
- **Rice:** Chinigura (Mymensingh/Sylhet), brown rice (local).
- **Honey:** Sundarban (wild), black seed honey.
- **Tea:** Kazi & Kazi (Rangpur/Mymensingh).
- **Veg/fruit:** Gazipur, Manikganj organic clusters.
- **Dairy:** Savar/Manikganj farms; Aarong Dairy.
- **Poultry/eggs:** organic poultry farms near Dhaka.
- **Superfoods:** Moringa (local native), Spirulina (local farms), Matcha (import via Organica).

## Regional Organic Agriculture Hubs (Direct Farm Sourcing)
- **Sylhet, Rajshahi, Chattogram, Mymensingh** — these regions act as organic agriculture hubs.
- **Rajshahi model:** Rajshahi farmers sell chemical-free mangoes directly to Dhaka via Facebook, bypassing middlemen. We can replicate this model for honey, rice, and veg — buying direct from farms to boost margin and traceability.

## Outreach Approach (Script)
1. Shortlist 3–5 candidates per category from the table.
2. First contact (WhatsApp/call): "We are launching an organic staples brand for Gulshan/Diplomatic Zone. Do you supply wholesale / white-label in bulk? Please share price list + MOQ + certs."
3. Visit top 1–2 farms/mills to verify quality, hygiene, certs.
4. Negotiate repack rights + credit terms.
5. Trial order (small batch), test packing + delivery in the zone.
6. Lock 1 primary + 1 backup supplier per category.

---

## Recommended Shortlist for Phase-1 Launch
- **Oils:** SUO XI or local kolhu mill (wholesale).
- **Ghee / Honey:** Khaas / Sunnah Bazar wholesale.
- **Rice / Atta:** Probortona / Khaas.
- **Superfoods:** Organica (import).
- **Tea:** Kazi & Kazi / SUO XI.
- **Dairy / Meat / Egg:** local farm + Khaas.

---

## Verify Before Signing
- Valid certs (BSTI / organic / halal).
- Consistent supply & lead time.
- MOQ & price breaks.
- Repack / resale permission (white-label).
- Food safety / hygiene audit.
- Confirm WE obtain BSTI CM (Conformity Mark) and Bangla labeling for our repacked products.
