# Phase-2 Kitchen & Financial Plan (Chef-Made Organic Meals)

> Step 5 of the plan. After Phase-1 staples demand is proven, add daily chef-made organic meals.

## Premise
Phase 2 turns the brand from "staples seller" into "daily organic food provider" using a licensed kitchen + chef. Build only after Phase-1 traction.

## Kitchen setup
- Licensed cloud kitchen / commissary near the zone (Gulshan/Baridhara).
- Food Safety (FSB) licence, BSTI where packaged, fire & trade licence.
- Zoned: raw veg, cooked, packaging; separate veg/non-veg; cold storage; hot-holding for delivery.
- Packaging: leak-proof, microwave-safe, labelled (Bangla) with ingredients, date, allergen.

## Chef & staff
- 1 head chef (organic/healthy cuisine) + 2 assistants + 1 kitchen helper + 1 rider (zone) + 1 order/customer manager.
- Hygiene training & health cards for all handlers.

## Menu (from sample-menu doc)
- Healthy Bengali (fish/veg curry, khichuri), Foreign (pasta, grill, salad), Keto/low-carb, Kids (mild, nutritious).
- Daily rotating menu; weekly subscription; customizable for allergy/diet.

## Model
- Subscription: weekly (5/6 days) and monthly plans; single-meal add-ons.
- Price points (estimate): lunch/dinner ৳350–550; kids meal ৳250–350; subscription discount 10–15%.
- Own rider inside zone; courier outside.

## Financial model (estimate, BDT)
One-time setup:
- Kitchen rent deposit + equipment (chiller, stove, prep): ৳3.0–5.0 lakh
- Licences & compliance: ৳0.5–1.0 lakh
- Initial packaging & branding: ৳0.3–0.5 lakh
- Total one-time: ~৳4.0–6.5 lakh

Monthly operating (at start):
- Staff salary: chef ৳40k + 2×৳25k + helper ৳18k + rider ৳20k + manager ৳35k = ৳1.63 lakh
- Rent: ৳0.6–1.0 lakh
- Ingredients + packaging per meal: ৳150–250
- Utilities & misc: ৳0.2 lakh

Break-even (example): at ৳450 avg price, ৳200 cost → ৳250 contribution/meal.
Fixed ~৳2.4 lakh/month → break-even ≈ 960 meals/month ≈ 32 meals/day.
With subscription of ~40 households × 5 days = 200 meals/week ≈ 860/month → profitable.

## Risk & next
- Validate meal interest in interviews (Step 4) before committing kitchen cost.
- Start with 1 cuisine, expand as orders grow.
