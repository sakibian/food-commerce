# PRD — Hyperlocal Premium Food Business (Gulshan · Baridhara · DOHS · Diplomatic Zone, Dhaka)

> Status: Draft v0.1 (brainstorming)
> Date: 2026-08-13
> Author: Founder / Team

---

## 0. Context & Demographic Snapshot

**Target geography:** A tightly concentrated, ultra-affluent pocket of Dhaka covering:

- **Baridhara Diplomatic Zone** (south-west, main residential plots)
- **Baridhara General Residential / J-Block**
- **Baridhara DOHS** (Army Welfare area)
- **Gulshan** (adjacent, similar income profile)

**Available (estimated) market data:**

| Area | Approx. Households | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Baridhara Diplomatic Zone only | **2,500 – 4,000 families** | 500–600 residential plots; each 5–7+ story building ~6–12 families |
| Whole Baridhara + Baridhara DOHS | **10,000 – 12,000 families** | Includes diplomatic + general residential + DOHS |
| Gulshan (adjacent) | not estimated | Add separately — large affluent population |

**Who lives there:** diplomats, foreign embassy staff, ambassadors, expatriates, ultra-HNI (UHNI) Bangladeshi business families. High disposable income, time-poor, quality- and safety-conscious, often with domestic staff but willing to pay for convenience/premium.

> ⚠️ Data caveat: No official family count is published. All figures are RAJUK/plot-density estimates. **Demand validation must be done on the ground before committing capital.**

---

## 1. Problem / Opportunity

- Affluent, time-poor households in this zone want reliable, high-quality, safe food but face:

  - Inconsistent quality from existing restaurants/caterers
  - Limited healthy / dietary-specific (halal, keto, diabetic, kids, expat-cuisine) options
  - Food-safety concerns with informal home cooks
  - Delivery that doesn't respect gated-community/security protocols

- Opportunity: a **trusted, premium, hyperlocal** food brand serving this small, dense, high-LTV customer base.

---

## 2. Vision & Goals (proposed)

- **Vision:** The default trusted food provider for embassy/gated-community households in the zone.
- **MVP goal (0–3 mo):** Win ~100–200 recurring households in Diplomatic Zone + DOHS.
- **6–12 mo:** Expand to full Baridhara + Gulshan; add catering/B2B (embassies, offices).
- **Unit economics goal:** Contribution margin ≥ 40% per order at scale.

---

## 3. Target Customer Segments

1. **Diplomatic / expat households** — need familiar cuisines, import-quality ingredients, discretion.
2. **UHNI Bangladeshi families** — daily meals, event catering, healthy meal plans.
3. **Working couples with kids** — daily tiffin / healthy kids meals.
4. **Corporate / embassy B2B** — office lunches, event catering.

---

## 4. Product / Service Offering (options to decide)

| Model | Description | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Cloud kitchen — daily meals** | Subscription + on-demand premium meals | High repeat, best LTV |
| **B. Specialty / healthy meal plans** | Keto, salad, protein, diabetic-friendly | Differentiates vs restaurants |
| **C. Catering & events** | Embassy/party catering | High margin, sporadic |
| **D. Gourmet packaged goods** | Bakery, frozen, sauces | Shelf-stable revenue |

**Recommendation:** Start with **A + B** (daily/healthy meals, cloud kitchen), layer **C** once trusted.

---

## 5. Business Model

- Subscription (weekly/monthly meal plans) + on-demand orders via WhatsApp/Instagram → lightweight app later.
- Delivery: own riders for the small zone (faster, secure gated access) or part-time.
- Pricing: premium tier (vs. mass food delivery).
- Payment: bKash / card / corporate invoicing.

---

## 6. Establishment / Setup Needed (focus areas)

**Legal & compliance**
- Trade license (city corp), VAT registration, halal certification, FSS (food safety) compliance, fire & hygiene license for kitchen. (See detailed Section 10.)
- If serving embassies: possible vendor registration / food handler certs.

**Kitchen & production**
- Licensed commercial kitchen (own or shared/cloud). Location near zone to cut delivery time.
- Cold storage, packaging (leak-proof, branded), hygiene SOPs, trained cooks.

**Team**
- Head chef, cooks, kitchen hygiene staff, delivery riders, 1 community/sales person for gated-community trust-building.

**Distribution & ops**
- Delivery routing for gated communities (security gate coordination, ID passes).
- Order system (start: WhatsApp/IG; build simple web order form).

**Brand & trust**
- Local word-of-mouth, embassy/community group presence, sampling, referrals.

**Capital & metrics**
- Estimate setup cost, break-even household count, CAC in a tight community.

## 8. Risks

- Small TAM in one zone → must expand to adjacent zones to scale.
- Gated-community access friction.
- High expectation bar (one bad meal loses a wealthy, vocal customer).
- Estimate-based demand (no official data) → validate before heavy spend.

## 9. Proposed Next Steps (MVP)

1. **Validate:** 15–20 customer interviews in Diplomatic Zone/DOHS; confirm cuisine + price.
2. **Pilot:** 1 shared kitchen, 1 chef, WhatsApp ordering, 30–50 trial households.
3. **Measure:** repeat rate, margin, delivery cost per order.

## 10. Legal & Licenses

The legal paperwork and licenses needed to start and run the business:

- **Trade License from City Corporation (DNCC/DSCC):** from Dhaka North/South City Corporation (our area of Gulshan/Baridhara falls under DNCC). Cost: BDT 5,000–15,000.
- **VAT registration (BIN) via NBR:** Business Identification Number via the National Board of Revenue. Cost: free.
- **BSTI Certification Mark (CM) license:** required for packaged/processed foods (honey, spices, mustard oil, rice if sold under our brand) from the Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI). Cost: BDT 10,000–30,000. Product label MUST be in Bangla (ingredients, manufacture date, expiry, weight); valid 3 years.
- **Bangladesh Safe Food Authority (BFSA) registration under Food Safety Act 2013:** register with BFSA under the Food Safety Act 2013. Cost: BDT 2,000–5,000. They inspect kitchen/hygiene (HACCP).
- **Fire license (Fire Service & Civil Defence):** fire-safety license from Fire Service & Civil Defence. Cost: BDT 8,000–20,000.
- **Halal certification (voluntary):** from BSTI or Islamic Foundation — not mandatory, but builds trust in this zone.
- **Company registration (RJSC) and TIN:** register the company via RJSC and obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN).

> 📌 Note: because we RE-PACK staples under our own brand, BSTI CM + Bangla labeling applies from Phase 1 (not only Phase 2 kitchen).
