# Sample Organic Menus & Required Items

> For Gulshan–Baridhara–DOHS–Diplomatic Zone. These are Phase-2 (chef-meal) plans; the ingredients double as our Phase-1 parcel-staple line.

---

## Common Organic Staple Inventory (source for ALL plans)

- Cold-pressed mustard oil
- Desi ghee
- Honey (black seed / sundarban)
- Organic rice (Chinigura, brown)
- Organic spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, nigella)
- Herbal teas (tulsi, moringa)
- Milk / yogurt (Bogra doi)
- Dates & nuts
- Superfood powders (moringa, spirulina) for fitness/keto
- Sea salt, black pepper

> Note: These same staples are what we will stock as the Phase-1 parcel line. Once repacked under our brand, they must carry the BSTI Certification Mark and Bangla labeling — this ties to the Phase-1 legal requirement.

---

## 1. Healthy Bangla Plan

**Concept:** Balanced organic Bangladeshi thali, low oil, high veg.

**Sample meal:** Chinigura rice + masoor dal + organic rui fish curry + spinach & gourd veg + salad + yogurt.

**Items needed:**
- Organic Chinigura rice
- Organic red lentil (masoor)
- Organic fish (rui, tilapia, pangas) or chicken
- Vegetables: spinach, bottle gourd, pumpkin, eggplant, okra
- Cold-pressed mustard oil
- Spices: turmeric, cumin, coriander, nigella, green chili, garlic, ginger, onion
- Salad: cucumber, tomato, carrot
- Organic yogurt

---

## 2. Expat / Continental Plan

**Concept:** Familiar Western meals for diplomats/expatriates — clean, mild, protein-rich.

**Sample meal:** Grilled organic chicken + quinoa/brown rice + roasted broccoli & zucchini + garden salad + olive-oil lemon dressing.

**Items needed:**
- Organic chicken breast / fish (local organic or imported)
- Quinoa / brown rice / wholegrain pasta
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Veg: broccoli, zucchini, carrot, bell pepper, lettuce, cherry tomato, asparagus
- Herbs: basil, rosemary, thyme, parsley
- Lemon, garlic, onion
- Salad greens, feta/cheese
- Wholegrain bread
- Organic eggs
- Sea salt, black pepper

---

## 3. Keto Plan

**Concept:** Low-carb, high-fat organic meals for fitness/weight management.

**Sample meal:** Avocado-egg salad + grilled chicken/beef + cauliflower rice + sautéed spinach in ghee + greek yogurt.

**Items needed:**
- Organic eggs
- Organic chicken, beef, fish
- Avocado (imported)
- Leafy greens: spinach, lettuce, kale
- Cheese, butter, ghee
- Olive oil / coconut oil
- Nuts & seeds (almond, walnut, chia, flax)
- Cauliflower, broccoli, zucchini
- Unsweetened greek yogurt
- Spices, salt, pepper
- Superfood powders (moringa, spirulina)

---

## 4. Kids Organic Plan

**Concept:** Soft, mild, nutrient-dense small portions for children.

**Sample meal:** Soft rice + minced organic chicken + mashed carrot/pumpkin + boiled egg + fruit + yogurt.

**Items needed:**
- Soft organic rice
- Organic chicken (minced)
- Mashed veg: potato, pumpkin, carrot, peas, beans
- Organic egg
- Fruit: banana, apple, pear
- Organic yogurt / curd
- Ghee (energy)
- Wholegrain bread / small roti
- Mild spices (little turmeric, cumin)

---

## Note

All four plans draw from the SAME organic staple inventory — so Phase-1 parcel staples directly feed Phase-2 meal cooking (vertical integration).
