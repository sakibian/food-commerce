# Customer Interview Script (Phase 1 Validation)

> Step 4 of the plan. Run 15–20 interviews in Gulshan / Baridhara / DOHS / Diplomatic Zone to confirm demand before investing.

## Objective
Validate: (a) willingness to buy parcel organic staples, (b) price sensitivity, (c) interest in future chef-made organic meals, (d) preferred ordering & delivery.

## Who to interview
- 15–20 households inside the target zone.
- Mix: diplomats/expat families, wealthy local families, health-conscious parents.
- Gate: get a referral or community intro; interview at home, by phone, or WhatsApp voice — keep 10–15 min.

## Script (ask open, then probe)
1. Where do you currently buy organic / "chemical-free" food?
2. Which organic products do you most need but struggle to find locally? (rice, dal, oil, honey, spices, veg, meat, ready meals?)
3. Would you buy pre-packed organic staples (rice, dal, honey, oil, masala) from a trusted new brand? Why / why not?
4. Roughly how much do you spend on groceries per week?
5. For a 1-kg organic rice pack, what price feels fair? (anchor ৳120–180); for raw honey 500g? (৳400–600)
6. How do you prefer delivery inside your gated community — our own rider at your door, or a courier at the gate?
7. Would you be interested in daily chef-made organic meals (lunch/dinner) later? Which type — Bengali, foreign, keto, kids?
8. Would you take a weekly or monthly meal subscription?
9. Which channel do you prefer to order — WhatsApp, Facebook/Instagram, or a website?
10. Which brands do you trust today for organic food, and why?
11. Would you pre-register for our launch offer (discount on first staples order)?

## How to record
Log in a spreadsheet with columns: date, household type, current source, top-needed product, staples yes/no, weekly grocery spend, acceptable rice price, delivery preference, meal interest (type), subscription yes/no, preferred channel, trusted brand, pre-register yes/no.

## Analysis
- % who will buy staples, average acceptable price, top 5 products.
- % interested in meals + preferred cuisine.
- Preferred channel & delivery → confirms Phase-1 online plan.
- If <50% staples interest, revisit product mix before building inventory.

## Target
Complete 15–20 interviews within 2 weeks; review before stocking inventory.
